<size=24><b>Shortcut Operations</b></size><br/>
Select the same unit twice in a row to automatically select all nearby units of the same type.<br/>
After selecting multiple units, click the ground to make them move while maintaining their original formation; double-click the same spot, and the units will converge at the target location.<br/>
You can set rally points for unit production in <color=#40a9ff>Command Centers, Barracks, Vehicle Factories, Airports</color> and <color=#ff4d4f> Hive</color>. Assign the rally point of Construction Vehicles or Drone Larvae to a resource node, and newly produced units will automatically collect resources there.<br/>
<outline color=#ffff00 width=1>Idle Construction Vehicles/Drone Larvae Button</outline>: A single click selects all idle units; a second click cycles through them one by one. If 5 seconds elapse between clicks, the next click will be treated as the first one.<br/>
<br/>
<size=24><b>Human Construction</b></size><br/>
<outline color=#ffffff width=1>Construct Buildings</outline>: Click the button for <color=#40a9ff>Command Center/Civilian House/Bunker/Barracks/Battery Turret</color> => click the ground. The nearest idle Construction Vehicle will move to the target location to build. If you select a Construction Vehicle first before initiating construction, that specific vehicle will be forced to perform the build task.<br/>
<outline color=#ffffff width=1>Produce Mobile Units</outline>: Click the button for <color=#40a9ff>Construction Vehicle/Soldier/Melee Infantry/Tank</color>. The least occupied production structure will start manufacturing the unit. If you select a Command Center or Barracks first, the unit will be produced in the specified structure.<br/>
Civilian Houses or Transport Drones increase the maximum population cap of mobile units.<br/>
A Bunker or Transport Drone can garrison up to 10 mobile units, and Bunkers have a very high health pool.<br/>

<color=#ff9500><b>Battery Turret</b></color>: Turret groups counter all units except Tanks<br/>

<size=24><b>Insectoids Construction</b></size><br/>
<outline color=#ffffff width=1>Construct Buildings</outline>: Insectoids buildings are created by mutating Drone Larvae, so each building constructed consumes one Drone Larva.<br/>
<outline color=#ffffff width=1>Produce Mobile Units</outline>: Insectoids mobile units hatch from Larvae. To speed up production, you must build more Hive.<br/>
<br/>
<size=24><b>Insectoids Unique Units</b></size><br/>
<color=#ff9500><b>PopuLifter Drone</b></color>: Can transport units<br/>

<size=24><b>Resource Gathering</b></size><br/>
Click to select a Construction Vehicle => click a <color=#b7b7a4>Mineral Field</color> or <color=#ff7a45>Vespene Geyser</color> to order resource collection.<br/>
Click the <outline color=#ffff00 width=1>Idle Construction Vehicles</outline> button to quickly locate all idle construction units.<br/>
Select a Command Center or Hive, then set the rally point of Construction Vehicles or Drone Larvae to a resource node. Newly produced units will head directly to collect resources upon spawning.<br/>
