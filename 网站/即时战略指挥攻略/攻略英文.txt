<size=24><b>Shortcut Operations</b></size><br/>
Select the same unit twice in a row to automatically select all nearby units of the same type.<br/>
After selecting multiple units, click the ground to make them move while maintaining their original formation; double-click the same spot, and the units will converge at the target location.<br/>
Normal movement makes combat units attack enemies they encounter, while Medics automatically heal nearby wounded allies. Use <color=#ffd666><b>Force Move</b></color> to ignore enemies and continue to the destination. You can also set patrol points, follow a target, or hold position.<br/>
You can set rally points for unit production in <color=#40a9ff>Command Centers, Barracks, Heavy Vehicle Factories, Airports</color> and <color=#ff4d4f>Hives</color>. Assign the rally point of Construction Vehicles or Worker Drones to a resource node, and newly produced workers will automatically collect resources there.<br/>
<color=#ffd666><b>Idle Construction Vehicles/Worker Drones Button</b></color>: A single click selects all idle workers; subsequent clicks cycle through them one by one. After the server-configured interval, the next click selects all idle workers again (10 seconds by default).<br/>

<size=24><b>Human Construction</b></size><br/>
<color=#ffd666><b>Construct Buildings</b></color>: Click the button for <color=#40a9ff>Command Center/Supply Depots/Bunker/Barracks/Turret/Airport/Heavy Vehicle Factory</color> => click the ground. The nearest idle Construction Vehicle will move to the target location to build. If you select a Construction Vehicle first, that vehicle will perform the build task. Some buildings have prerequisites.<br/>
<color=#ffd666><b>Produce Mobile Units</b></color>: Command Centers produce Construction Vehicles and Medics; Barracks produce Riflemen and Melee Infantry; Heavy Vehicle Factories produce Tanks and Mechs; Airports produce Aircraft and Anti-Air Troopers. Select one or more structures that can produce the unit to use them; otherwise production is assigned to an eligible structure with a shorter queue. Some units require prerequisite buildings or must be unlocked first.<br/>
Barracks, Bug Barracks, Heavy Vehicle Factories, Airports, Air Towers and Mimic Sources provide different unit upgrades. Select a structure to see its available upgrades.<br/>
Completed Command Centers add 6 population, Hives add 5, Supply Depots add 8, and PopuLifter Drones add 6. The default per-player population cap is 100; check the in-game counter for the active limit.<br/>
A Bunker or PopuLifter Drone can garrison up to 10 mobile units. Select units and left-click the Bunker or PopuLifter Drone to load them; right-click it to unload them. If a PopuLifter Drone is destroyed, its passengers are released with 1 HP. Bunkers have a very high health pool.<br/>

<size=24><b>Hero Unit: Mech</b></size><br/>
<color=#ff9500><b>Mechs</b></color> are produced by Heavy Vehicle Factories. Their progression is different from building-based unit upgrades: a Mech that contributes to an enemy kill earns experience based on its share of the damage. When its experience is full, it levels up automatically, increases and fully restores its health, and gains one <color=#ffd666><b>skill point</b></color>.<br/>
Each level-up presents skill choices. Spend skill points on <color=#ff3b30>Rocket</color>, <color=#5ac8fa>Light Spiker</color>, <color=#ff9500>Flame</color>, or <color=#34c759>Range</color>. These points apply only to that Mech; they are not bought from buildings and cannot be reset.<br/>
<color=#ff3b30><b>Rocket</b></color>: Fires in a straight line toward the current target for reliable damage. Upgrades increase projectile count, range, and flight speed; high levels pierce additional enemies.<br/>
<color=#5ac8fa><b>Light Spiker</b></color>: Automatically homes in on nearby enemies and acquire a new target if the current one is lost. Upgrades improve range, flight speed, and piercing; high levels also fire more Light Spikers in succession.<br/>
<color=#ff9500><b>Flame</b></color>: Sprays a cone of fire toward the nearest enemy, applying damage over time to enemies in the area. Upgrades expand its range and cone angle while shortening the interval between casts. It is especially effective against grouped enemies.<br/>
<color=#34c759><b>Range</b></color>: Increases the range of Rocket, Light Spiker, and Flame at the same time. It pairs well with any offensive skill for safer long-range damage.<br/>

<size=24><b>Insectoids Construction</b></size><br/>
<color=#ffd666><b>Construct Buildings</b></color>: Insectoids buildings are created by mutating Worker Drones, so each building constructed consumes one Worker Drone. All Insectoids buildings except Hives must be placed on Mossy Vines; buildings off Mossy Vines continuously lose health.<br/>
<color=#ffd666><b>Produce Mobile Units</b></color>: Insectoids mobile units hatch from Larvae. Each Hive can support up to 3 Larvae and produces about one every 10 seconds; more Hives increase Larva supply.<br/>

<size=24><b>Resource Gathering</b></size><br/>
Click to select a Construction Vehicle => click a <color=#b7b7a4>Mineral Field</color> or <color=#ff7a45>Vespene Geyser</color> to order resource collection.<br/>
Workers automatically travel between a resource node and the nearest Command Center or Hive to deposit resources. Resource nodes are finite and disappear when depleted.<br/>
Click the <color=#ffd666><b>Idle Construction Vehicles/Worker Drones</b></color> button to quickly locate idle workers.<br/>
Select a Command Center or Hive, then set the Construction Vehicle or Worker Drone rally point on a resource node. Newly produced workers will head directly there and begin gathering.<br/>
